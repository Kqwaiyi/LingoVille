# Insomniacs

A low-poly 3D life sim in the browser where you learn a language by living in a town whose NPCs only respond when you speak their language.

## The Unique Kernel
You don't study the language; you need it to get through the day. Ordering food, paying rent and working a café Shift only succeed if the NPC understood you, and the NPCs quietly adapt to a Language Proficiency the player never sees.

## Who It's For
Someone who has a long streak in a drills app but freezes when they actually have to speak. They've done the studying; what they're missing is somewhere safe to say it out loud and be understood.

## The Core Loop
Walk up to someone in town, press E, and speak in the Target Language to get something done. Shifts earn money, money pays for food, rent and the doctor, and Well-being and Mood have to stay up. Each conversation ends in a short Recap with up to three corrections.
Why they come back: needs run down and rent comes due every week, so every day takes more conversations.

## Inspiration & Identity
A free-roaming town in the spirit of Bloxburg, in cozy pastel low-poly.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
Not established.

## What "Working" Looks Like
All three of these moments:
1. They walk into the café, ask for a coffee out loud in the Target Language, and the barista reads the order back and hands it over. The Recap shows one correction.
2. They say something the NPC doesn't understand, the NPC asks again, they use a Help hint, and the second try works.
3. They work one barista Shift: customers order out loud, they make the drinks, and the pay at the end depends on how many they got right.

Moments 1 and 2 can be a single café scene (fail, hint, succeed, Recap) to keep the video near a minute.

## The POC Boundary
- Speaking to NPCs to complete goals, with Help and the Recap.
- The barista Job and its Shifts.
- The gym.
- Illness, the clinic and the hospital bill.
- Rent and debt.
- All four languages: Japanese, Chinese, English and German.

## Later
None named.

## Explicitly Cut
- Gifts and Familiarity: the demo doesn't need them.
- The cashier and server Jobs: the demo doesn't need them.
- Multiplayer, house building, accounts and cloud saves, mobile, and languages beyond the four: out of scope from the start. The game runs locally on the developer's own Gemini key.
