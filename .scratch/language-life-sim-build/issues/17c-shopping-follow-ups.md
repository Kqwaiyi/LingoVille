# 17c — Shopping follow-ups: fainting with a basket, can't afford, asking with shopping, mock read-back

**What to build:** Close the gaps the 17a review found in how shopping behaves. A basket must never survive Fainting. A Player who can't afford their shopping must have a way forward. The Player must be able to ask where something is even with shopping in the basket. And the mock cashier must read back the total, as the real one does.

**Blocked by:** 17a — Supermarket and convenience store: groceries, inventory, finding an item and counter food

**Spec:** [spec.md](../spec.md): Goal Interactions (Flow step 4, #4, #5); user story 53 (read-back with the price)

**Status:** done

The mock gets the basket through the session (FACTS), or through a structured field added to `NpcSession`. Test it in all four packs (`mockVoiceSession.test.ts`), and update `e2e/groceries.spec.ts`.

## Comments

- **Fainting:** `fainted` in the store now empties the basket and clears the shelf marker itself. The failing test came first: the Character was in the clinic with the eggs still in the basket.
- **Can't afford (decided: put back at the till):** the spec's Flow says "the conversation continues" and story 55 says "so that I can order something cheaper", so the Player can now put shopping back mid-conversation at the till, until it's paid for. `selectCanPutBack` holds the rule for both `putBack` and the basket's "−" buttons: any time outside a conversation, and at the till before the outcome while the cashier can hear about it (not while the Player is talking, Help is open or the connection is coming back). The last item stays on the counter, because leaving the till is how to put everything back, and an empty basket would only get `invalid_arguments`. Each put-back starts a new NPC turn and updates `conversation.basket`, which is what `complete_purchase` charges for. It also sends the cashier `basketChangedScene` (in `ai`), a `[SCENE: …]` with the new shopping and total that replaces the shopping in FACTS. A replacement session after a dropped connection is rebuilt from the basket as it is now (`SessionFor` in the store). The cashier's cannot_afford line now says "they can put something back" (no "come again").
- **#5 with shopping (decided: F at the cashier):** there's still one staff role per counter. With shopping in the basket, E at the cashier pays and F asks where something is (`interactionStartedWithF` in `content/approaches.ts`). The prompt reads "Press E to pay — Cashier · Press F to ask where something is". F does nothing anywhere else. The store exposes `selectTalkWithE` and `selectTalkWithF`, and `InteractionPrompt` picks "pay" from E's interaction (`effect.kind === 'purchase'`). That removes the duplicated "with shopping, the cashier means pay" rule from 17a's tidy-up list.
- **Mock read-back:** the mock cashier reads the total from the session text, the same "Total: …" line the real model gets in FACTS (`readBasketTotal` in `content/facts.ts`, next to `basketFacts`, which writes it). A later basket scene replaces it. A put-back before the questions are answered carries on with the current question, without greeting again. After cannot_afford it keeps the bag and card choices, asks whether the Player wants to put something back, and reads back the new total once something is put back.
- **Not done:** `npm run eval` hasn't been run for the changed cashier prompt or the new scene, because it needs real Gemini.
- **Review leftovers (judgement calls, not done):** `readBasketTotal` is a parser in `content`, which is "data plus schemas". It sits next to `basketFacts`, which writes the line, so rewording one breaks the mock's total tests instead of failing silently. `effect.kind === 'purchase'` now also means "the till" in `selectCanPutBack` and `InteractionPrompt`; an `isPurchase` predicate would join the effect-behaviour map from 17a's tidy-ups.
- **Seen during the full e2e run:** `readingAids.spec.ts` "Show romaji adds a romaji line" failed once (the Show romaji checkbox was still unchecked after `keepSettings`), then passed 3/3 alone. Nothing here touches settings, so it looks like a timing problem in that test's setup.
