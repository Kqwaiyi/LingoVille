# 31a — Kit art, palette and fog

**What to build:** The greybox town becomes a cozy pastel low-poly town built from kit assets, recoloured to one shared palette, with flat shading and gentle distance fog.

**Blocked by:** 13b — Greybox town of 11 places and tram travel

**Spec:** [spec.md](../spec.md): Art and audio

**Status:** done

- [x] Kenney and Quaternius (CC0) assets replace the greybox for all 11 places, recoloured to one shared palette of about 24 colours.
- [x] Flat shading and gentle distance fog. No weather.
- [x] Façades, roofs and layout are shared by every pack.

## Comments

- **The palette.** `src/world/palette.ts` holds 27 named colours: neutrals, woods, pastel façades, greens and a few accents (teal, mustard, denim, plum, red, lamplight). Every colour in `src/world` is one of these names: kit art, aprons, signs, props, groceries, the marker, the sky and fog, and the light colours. Aprons and light colours are snapped to their nearest palette colour, so roles and lighting look as they did. `tooling/townArt.test.ts` fails any `#hex` literal in `src/world` outside `palette.ts`. **Not on the palette:** skin tones and hair colours, which tint grey textures from `src/content/appearance.ts` as 30a left them, and the character eye texture.
- **Art source.** Kenney's CC0 kits: Building Kit (walls, doors, floors, flat roofs, columns), Modular Buildings (gable roofs), City Kit Roads (road, street lights, stop pole), Train Kit (rails), Furniture Kit and Nature Kit. `npm run build:town -- <folder>` bakes the pieces the town asks for (`TOWN_PIECES` in `townArt.ts`) into `public/town/town.glb` (≈0.35 MB): in metres, standing on the ground, centred on their footprint, front to +z. The kits are too big for the repo; the script's header says where to get them (each from its kenney.nl page). The Quaternius side is the character rig from 30a.
- **Recolouring.** Each triangle takes the colour under its middle (the kit's colour map, or its material colour), snapped to the nearest palette colour in OKLab (`scripts/palette.ts`). One flat material per palette colour, named after it, with no textures; window glass keeps its own `window` material. The Building Kit's colour map shades faces from gradients, so the build settles each kind of piece on one colour per surface (`repaint` maps): walls come out in `KIT_FACADE` outside and cream inside, and roofs in `KIT_ROOF`. Each building repaints those two with its own façade and roof colour (`BUILDINGS` in `town.ts`). The Furniture and Nature kits store sRGB colours where glTF expects linear, which the build allows for. The nature kit's aqua greens are made leaf green.
- **The outfits** (handed over by 30a). `build:characters` now repaints the peasant outfit texture from the palette: smoothed, then each texel ranked by lightness onto a ramp of ink → walnut → wood → sand → linen, lossless. Plain nearest-colour snapping turned the dark painted browns to ink and the shirt to slate, so it ranks by lightness instead. Skin and hair still tint grey textures from the content's colours, as 30a left them. `tooling/townArt.test.ts` checks every outfit texel is a palette colour.
- **Buildings.** Walls are runs of 2 m wall pieces stretched slightly to fit each wall, with a window in the middle of each run beside the door, and the kit's wide doorway narrowed to the 1.8 m door. Walls, closed doors and furniture keep their colliders: `Solid` boxes from the same layout, drawn by whatever stands there. The closed-door leaves, the staff doors and the furniture are kit pieces too. Furniture fills its old collider box, repeated along long boxes (counters, aisles, bookcases) and facing the way `FURNITURE` says; `height` lets a bed's headboard or a bench's back stand taller than what's solid. `DECOR` adds rugs, plants and fridges with no collider; 31c may move any of these into the per-pack set dressing.
- **Roofs.** Five buildings have gable roofs and four have flat roofs with a low parapet in the façade colour. A roof lifts off whenever it stands between the camera and the Character, which includes the Character being inside. On the title screen it lifts off the save's place. Without this, the new roofs hid the Character behind buildings, which the open-topped greybox never did. The café's name sign moved out to hang in front of the eaves (z 3.32), so the roof doesn't cover it.
- **Outdoors.** The street is laid with road tiles, with rails down the middle. Island platforms have a stop pole and a shelter. Street lights stand along both pavements, clear of doors and stops, ready for 31b to light at dusk. **New colliders:** the twelve street-light poles and the shelters' two posts on each platform (on its back edge, clear of where the tram sets the Character down). The park has three kinds of tree, bushes and flowers around its edges, clear of the way in.
- **No kit has a running machine**, so `treadmill` is assembled in the build from kit parts (a road tile for the belt, columns for the posts and rail, a furniture screen for the console).
- **Dropped:** shop awnings. The commercial kit's "awning" is a canopy on posts and read as a bench on the façades. 31c could add per-pack awnings if wanted.
- **Flat shading** comes from the shared palette materials (`flatShading`); the build also writes flat per-face normals. **Fog** is the sky colour from 35 m to 95 m (`FOG` in `Scene.tsx`), up from 30–70 m, so the street stays clear.
- **For 31b:** window glass is the `window` material, lit (lamplight, emissive) while the place is open and charcoal while it's closed, per `Piece`'s `lit`. The street lights' bulbs are the `white` parts of `street-light`. Light colours now come from the palette (`cream`/`grass` hemisphere).
- **For 31c:** `Props` and `Groceries` are still primitive stand-ins, now in palette colours.
- **Smoke fix.** `e2e/directions.spec.ts` stepped aside off the pole for 150 ms. That step took the Character anywhere from 0.73 m to 0.9 m off the platform's middle line, depending on frame timing. Past 0.8 m it is off the 1.6 m platform, where no one at the stop is in reach. Even on it, the Character slid past the passer-by, so the prompt showed only briefly (the same at HEAD). The heavier scene tipped it into failing. It now steps 50 ms, slides round the pole and walks into the passer-by.
