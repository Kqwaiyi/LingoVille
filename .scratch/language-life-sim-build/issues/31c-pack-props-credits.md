# 31c — Per-pack props and credits

**What to build:** Each Culture Pack dresses the shared buildings with its own signs, menus, food, money and small props, using real assets instead of placeholders.

**Blocked by:** 31a — Kit art, palette and fog

**Spec:** [spec.md](../spec.md): Art and audio (Per-pack art, Sourcing)

**Status:** done

- [x] Per-pack props swap across all 11 places; façades, roofs and layout stay shared.
- [x] Signs and menus still come from pack strings, and pointing at them still shows their reading aid and Translate.
- [x] `CREDITS.md` lists any CC-BY assets.

## Comments

- **Content.** A pack's `props` is now a list per place (`Record<PlaceId, PropId[]>`), and `shelves` says which prop shows each grocery on the supermarket's shelf. The schema wants all 11 places, each with at least one prop. `culturePackProblems` fails a place dressed just as another pack dresses it (in any order), so every place swaps. `PROP_IDS` (`culturePacks.ts`) lists the 61 props the art has.
- **What each pack sets out.** Money at every till, as each country pays: a coin tray (ja, as its customs say), a QR code stand (zh), a contactless card reader (en) and a dish for change (de). At the door: noren (ja: café, restaurant, bathhouse), red couplets and lanterns (zh), bunting (en) and window boxes of geraniums (de), on most of the shops and the town office. Food and goods: onigiri, tea eggs, sausage rolls or Bockwurst at the convenience store's counter; teishoku, dim sum, fish and chips or sauerkraut and sausage on the restaurant's tables; a lucky cat, a tea set, a cake stand or a bread basket at the café; cabbages, bok choy, carrots or potatoes, and udon, dried noodles, spaghetti or Spätzle on the shelves; a mikan crate, rice sacks, flower buckets or drinks crates inside the supermarket's door. Small touches: a nabe, a steamer, a fry-up or a pan of sausages on the stove at home, with an andon, lucky bamboo, a radio or a teddy bear by the bed; a bonsai, a water dispenser, a magazine table or a coat stand in the clinic's waiting room; wash buckets, foot basins, a towel stack or a sauna bucket at the bathhouse; a hanami mat and a stone lantern, a xiangqi table, a picnic blanket or a beer bench in the park; and on each tram platform a vending machine, sorting bins, a post box or a Litfaßsäule. Medicine, books and paperwork are on the counters in every pack; eggs look the same on every pack's shelf.
- **Where props stand** is the world's: `src/world/setDressing.ts` keeps the shared spots, by kind (door, counter, table, floor, lawn, platform, shelf). `setOut` puts each prop in the next free spot of its kind at its place. `tooling/setDressing.test.ts` fails a pack whose props have no spot left, or a grocery shown as something other than a shelf prop. Props have no colliders, like `DECOR`: the Character walks through the bigger ones (a vending machine, a beer bench). A collider per prop would have to stay clear of every walk the smoke takes.
- **Art.** The props are nodes of `town.glb` next to the town's pieces (`ART_PIECES` = `TOWN_PIECES` + `PROP_IDS`), built by `npm run build:town` from `scripts/town-props.ts`. Two more Kenney CC0 kits are sources: the Food Kit (food, dishes, pots, packaging) and the Fantasy Town Kit (stools, benches, pointed roofs). Nothing in any kit is a lucky cat, a noren, a vending machine, a post box or a Litfaßsäule, so they are assembled from kit parts: `slab` (a floor tile as a box), `drum` (a tin), coins as flattened plates, bunting as flipped pointed roofs. A part can now be `paint`ed one palette colour, or `flip`ped upside down. `tooling/townArt.test.ts` now fails art without a prop. `town.glb` grows from 0.32 MB to 2.5 MB (about 39,000 triangles, mostly Food Kit models).
- **Signs.** Every place has a name board from the pack: over each building's door (the apartment block's name at home), on two posts at the park's way in, and on each tram stop's pole. Each place that keeps hours behind a door has them beside it, and the restaurant has a menu board (its dishes and drinks, with local prices) on its back wall, like the café's. Sign ids are `<place>-name`, `<stop>-name`, `<place>-hours` and `<place>-menu`. A one-line sign with a note (opening hours) now paints the time on its own row: before, a long word pushed the time off the board's left edge.
- **Name boards lift off with the roof.** With one on every building, the board over the door hid the inside from the follow camera whenever the Character was in. A board now hides while the Character is inside its building (and, on the title screen, over the save's place, as the roof does). While hidden it doesn't take the pointer, so the menu behind can still be pointed at.
- **`spawnPoint`** moved from `town.ts` into `Character.tsx`, its only user, so the tooling tests can import the layout without the store and Vite's `import.meta.env`.
- **Credits.** `CREDITS.md` lists every art source: all CC0 (Kenney's kits, Quaternius's characters), so its CC-BY section is empty. Ticket 33a's credits screen should read from the same list.
- **Smoke.** `e2e/culturePacks.spec.ts` adds pointing at a sign outdoors, from the pavement outside the bookshop in the zh pack: pinyin, then Translate. Any sign under the pointer counts (the bookshop's name or hours, or the tram stop's name), all of them new. Checked by screenshots of all 11 places in all four packs.
