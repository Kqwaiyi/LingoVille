# 31 — Cozy pastel art and day–night lighting

**What to build:** The greybox town becomes a cozy pastel low-poly town. Days visibly pass through morning, midday, golden hour and night. Windows and street lights come on at dusk. Each Culture Pack swaps signs, menus, food, money and small props on shared buildings.

**Blocked by:** 13 — The whole town: 11 places, hours and trams

**Spec:** [spec.md](../spec.md): Art and audio

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Kenney and Quaternius (CC0) assets, recoloured to one shared palette of about 24 colours, with flat shading and gentle distance fog.
- [ ] Four keyframed lighting presets, blended by sun angle and colour.
- [ ] Windows and street lights switch on at dusk, and a lit window still means a place is open.
- [ ] Per-pack props swap; façades, roofs and layout are shared. No weather.
- [ ] `CREDITS.md` lists any CC-BY assets.
